# G2211 Rules

## Core Requirements
All requirements below must be met:

A. Longitudinal relationship
- Ongoing or newly assumed responsibility for chronic disease management.
- Evidence of review of prior care, transition records, or disease trajectory.
- Intent for continued follow-up or management.

Note: A longitudinal relationship may begin in this encounter if the clinician explicitly assumes ongoing responsibility for chronic conditions.

B. Active chronic-care management today
- Treatment decisions, medication initiation/adjustment, or documented risk-based deferral with clinical reasoning.
- Not history-only or screening-only.

C. Inherent complexity
- Chronic or interacting conditions that require risk mitigation, constrained treatment options, or future planning.

D. Not acute-only, not one-off, not consult-only
- The visit is not limited to an acute self-limited issue or a one-time consultation without ongoing responsibility.

## Non-Qualifying Scenarios
Hard NOs:
- Chronic conditions listed without management.
- Screening, labs, or counseling only.
- Simple refills without oversight or management decisions.
- Patient refusal without documented clinical risk assessment.
- Acute/self-limited problems only.
- One-time specialty consultation with no assumption of ongoing care.

## Canonical Qualifying Patterns
The patterns below satisfy longitudinal evidence, chronic-care management, and inherent complexity when clearly documented:

1) Longstanding atrial fibrillation with stroke-risk management
- Chronic AF with review of prior care.
- Initiation or discussion of antithrombotic/cardioprotective therapy or future anticoagulation planning.
- Longitudinal follow-up planned.

2) Risk-constrained chronic symptom management
- Persistent, functionally significant symptoms with longitudinal history.
- Treatment options evaluated or deferred due to patient-specific risk.
- Ongoing responsibility for reassessment.

3) Establish-care or Transition-of-Care with chronic disease ownership
- Explicit statements such as “establish care”, “re-establish care”, “transition of care”, or post-discharge follow-up.
- Review of prior records or chronic disease history.
- Medication reconciliation, continuation, or modification.
- Assumption of ongoing responsibility and follow-up planning.

## Decision Rule
Assign G2211 = YES only if all are true:
1. Not acute-only.
2. Longitudinal evidence present.
3. Chronic-care management performed today.
4. Inherent complexity present.
5. Not a one-off consult-only encounter.

Administrative “new patient” status alone does not prohibit G2211 if longitudinal care responsibility is clearly established.

If any requirement is not met, return NO G2211.
