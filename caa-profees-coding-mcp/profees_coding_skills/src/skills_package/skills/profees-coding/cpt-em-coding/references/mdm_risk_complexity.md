# MDM Risk of Complications

Classify risk based on management actions documented for this encounter.

## Minimal
- Minimal risk of morbidity from additional diagnostic testing or treatment.
- Examples: gargles, elastic bandages, rest, or other minimal‑risk testing or treatment.

## Low
- Low risk of morbidity from additional diagnostic testing or treatment.
- Examples: OTC medications, physical therapy, occupational therapy, IV fluids without additives, minor surgery with no identified risk factors, or other low‑risk treatment.

## Moderate
- Moderate risk of morbidity.
- Examples: prescription drug management, decision regarding minor surgery with identified risk factors, decision regarding elective major surgery without identified risk factors, or management limited by social determinants of health.

## High
- High risk of morbidity.
- Examples: drug therapy requiring intensive monitoring for toxicity, decision for elective major surgery with risk factors, emergency major surgery, decision for hospitalization or escalation to hospital-level care, or decision to de-escalate care due to poor prognosis.

## General Rules
- Focus on management actions taken during this encounter, not hypothetical future actions.
- Count risk only when the provider documents a same-day directive in Assessment/Plan or Orders, such as start, stop, change, continue, no change, procedure decision, or escalation.
- Diagnosis alone does not establish risk.
- Base classification on explicit documentation only.
