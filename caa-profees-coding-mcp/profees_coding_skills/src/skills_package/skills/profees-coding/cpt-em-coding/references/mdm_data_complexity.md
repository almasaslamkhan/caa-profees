# MDM Data Complexity

Use these definitions to classify the Amount and/or Complexity of Data Reviewed and Analyzed.

## Minimal
- Minimal or no data to review.

## Low
- Meets at least one of the following:
- Any combination of two items: review of prior external notes from each unique source, review of results of each unique test, or ordering of each unique test.
- Assessment requiring an independent historian.

## Moderate
- Meets at least one of the following:
- Any combination of three items: review of prior external notes from each unique source, review of results of each unique test, ordering of each unique test, or assessment requiring an independent historian.
- Independent interpretation of a test performed by another physician or qualified health professional.
- Discussion of management or test interpretation with another qualified health care professional.

## High
- Meets at least two of the following:
- Any combination of three items: review of prior external notes from each unique source, review of results of each unique test, ordering of each unique test, or assessment requiring an independent historian.
- Independent interpretation of a test performed by another qualified health care professional.
- Discussion of management or test interpretation with an external physician or other qualified health professional.

## Counting Rules
- Each unique test, order, or document counts as one item.
- Count unique sources for external notes.
- Independent historian counts as a separate data element.
- A de-identified placeholder for an Independent historian, such as a redacted parent, spouse, or other historian reference, still counts when the note shows history came from that source.
- Laboratory panels, such as CMP or chemistry panels, count as a single test/order under AMA E/M rules; do not split panels into component elements.
